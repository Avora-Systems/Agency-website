Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Avora Systems ('we', 'us') respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the message you send us. We don't collect any other personal data through this site, and we don't use tracking cookies or analytics that identify you personally.

How we use it

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, except the tools we use to run our business (such as our email provider) to the extent needed to reply to you.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and maintain our own business records, and delete them on request.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact tom@avora-systems.com for any of these requests.
